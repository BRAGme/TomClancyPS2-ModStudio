Jungle Storm -- LAN co-op config
================================

This is the whole setup, 3 KB of it. It makes YOUR copy of Jungle Storm match Tristan's, and
points its ONLINE menu at his machine instead of Ubisoft's dead servers.

You need
  * Tom Clancy's Ghost Recon: Jungle Storm (USA), SLUS-20820, as a disc image
  * Tom Clancys PS2 ModStudio.exe
  * Radmin VPN, connected to the same network as Tristan
Nothing else. Do not send or download a patched disc image -- you patch your own.

Step 1 -- put the config next to your disc
------------------------------------------
Easiest, if you have Python:

    python install-config.py "D:\path\to\your Jungle Storm.iso"

No Python? Do it by hand instead. Beside your disc image, make these folders:

    <folder with your .iso>\.tcms-backup\<your iso's name without .iso>\

so a disc at  D:\Games\Jungle Storm.iso  wants

    D:\Games\.tcms-backup\Jungle Storm\

and copy applied-settings.json into it. The folder name must match the file name exactly,
without the .iso.

Step 2 -- load it in ModStudio
------------------------------
Open ModStudio, choose your disc, and press LAST APPLIED at the bottom. It will be greyed out
until step 1 is done. Pick "2026-09-28  LAN co-op", press Load, then press APPLY TO DISC.

It lists everything it is about to change before it writes anything, and it keeps a backup of
the original, so RESTORE DISC always puts your copy back.

Step 3 -- play
--------------
Tristan runs the lobby server on his PC. You just boot the patched disc and choose ONLINE.
There is no login to make: any name and password is accepted.

What the config turns on
------------------------
106 settings, the same ones Tristan is running, which matters: both copies need the same weapon
and kit data or the two games will not agree about what anyone is holding. Among them:

  * ONLINE points at 26.209.25.36 -- Tristan's Radmin address -- and skips the dead DNAS check
  * the shotgun, with its Heroes Unleashed numbers and its Sum of All Fears firing sound
  * smoke grenades, Heroes Unleashed weapon and skill values, third-person camera, 60 FPS
  * blood, bullet holes, callouts, penetration, louder enemy grenades

If Tristan's Radmin address ever changes, he sends you a new applied-settings.json and you redo
steps 1 and 2. Nothing else has to change.
